# Checklist (short version)

Replace this file with your full checklist, in any language. The evaluation step
reads it; the generation step deliberately never sees it.

## Phase 0: Kill gates (all five must pass)

1. **The mechanism is physically possible.** What causal chain leads from available input to the promised output?
2. **The advantage is not neutralised institutionally.** Law, market design, professional rules or platform policy.
3. **Money is already being spent on this.** Cash to third parties (strong), payroll (moderate), owner's own time (weak).
   Owner's time alone does not pass.
4. **I can talk to 10 potential customers within two weeks.** Channel, number of attempts, why they would reply.
5. **Someone pays upfront for a manual version.** A refusal on value is structural; on trust or handover it is
   solvable. What must the customer give up before getting value?

## Phase 1: Mechanism

- What is *not* in the data? Separate what you measure from what you assume.
- Coverage of the customer's job, independent of accuracy.
- Absolute number or distribution? Choose the weakest claim that still does the job.
- Feedback latency: seconds, days or months?
- Retrospective validation: 1 = public historical data, 2 = one customer's data, 3 = prospective only.
- Commission versus omission: what happens when something is missing?

## Founder fit

- Unfair advantage, own problem or n=1, the daily work in year two.
- Conflict of interest with the employer, its clients or its competitors.
